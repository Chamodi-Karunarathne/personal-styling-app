### One thing I'd strongly recommend

Don't write your system requirements around individual situations like:

> "If girls outing + public transport → don't recommend revealing clothes."

That will create **hundreds or thousands of rules** and become impossible to maintain.

Instead, create **generalizable attributes**:

```text
EVENT
├── formality
├── social_context
├── trend_relevance
└── activity_level

ENVIRONMENT
├── temperature
├── humidity
├── UV
├── rain
└── indoor/outdoor

MOBILITY
├── walking
├── transportation
├── duration
└── movement_required

USER
├── style
├── comfort
├── color
├── clothing_preferences
└── wardrobe

                    ↓

             STYLING ENGINE

                    ↓

             OUTFIT SCORE
```

That architecture will let **one rule work across many situations**, which makes your project much more scalable and technically impressive.
